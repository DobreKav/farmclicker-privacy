3. Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google LLC. When you first launch the app, you will be shown a consent dialog (required in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland under GDPR). You can choose whether to allow personalized ads.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Manage ad preferences: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Opt out of personalized ads: https://optout.aboutads.info

4. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not store any user data on our servers. Game progress is stored only on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. For AdMob data, contact Google at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you have the right to:

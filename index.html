3. Advertising, Analytics, and Attribution

Since version 4.3.1.6, Appodeal provides advertising mediation for the game, with BidMachine among the included demand partners. No ad is requested or shown before you accept this Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions on the game's first screen; when the policy changes, the game asks you to accept it again before ads start. Appodeal and the advertising demand partners enabled for an ad request may process advertising identifiers, IP/network and device information, diagnostics, app activity, and ad interaction data to select and serve ads, measure performance, prevent invalid activity, and report results. The specific demand partners can vary. Current builds do not package standalone AdMob or Unity Ads SDKs; provider configurations may differ in older or later releases.

Google Firebase Analytics may process pseudonymous app-instance identifiers and app/game events to help us understand stability and usage. Since version 4.3.4.2, the game includes AppsFlyer and the Meta (Facebook) SDK, started by Appodeal, to attribute installs from our advertising campaigns. They start only after you accept this Privacy Policy in the game, and may process installation, launch, event, advertising-ID, device and network information for attribution and measurement.

Where required by law, Appodeal's consent flow is presented when the SDK determines it is needed. You can also review or reset your Android advertising ID in your device settings. These controls may affect personalized advertising but do not necessarily stop all ads or processing needed for contextual ads, security, and measurement. To change a privacy choice or submit a rights request, contact NeonLabGames using the email below; this build does not currently provide a separate in-game button to reopen privacy choices.

4. Game Progress, Referrals, Purchases, and Notifications

Game progress is stored on your device. Optional Google Play Games features may provide cloud backup. Referral and promotional features may process a referral code, a pseudonymous account or installation identifier, and reward receipts/status to validate and deliver rewards. The referral anti-abuse value is an app-specific hash; the raw Android ID is not sent for that hash. Google Play Billing handles purchases; purchase information is used to grant, restore, and validate the purchased item.

Optional local notifications are scheduled on your device after you enable them and grant Android notification permission. Their schedule and content are not uploaded for notification delivery. You can turn them off in the game or Android settings.

5. Service Providers and Data Sharing

NeonLabGames does not sell your name or contact details. Information may be processed by the providers listed above and by their subprocessors or enabled advertising partners to provide ads, analytics, attribution, billing, cloud-save, security, and game services. Each provider's policy explains its processing, retention, and available controls. Some older app versions or configurations may have used additional advertising or analytics providers; this policy describes the current Appodeal-enabled version and identifies version-dependent processing where relevant.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Local game data and notification schedules can generally be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the game. Optional cloud saves, purchase, referral, analytics, advertising, and attribution data may be held by the relevant provider under its retention rules. For a privacy request concerning data controlled by NeonLabGames, contact us below and include the app version and a description of your request. Do not email passwords or full payment details.

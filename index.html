3. Advertising and Analytics Services

The app uses Google AdMob for the bottom banner and optional rewarded videos and Google Firebase Analytics for app and monetization analytics. AdMob may invite approved server-side bidding partners, including Bidease, to compete for an impression. A random, non-personal player ID is used to associate game and ad events across sessions on the same app installation. Google, Bidease and other advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived location, app/device details, ad interactions, and estimated impression value. Where required, users are offered consent and opt-out choices before advertising is requested.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Bidease Privacy Policy: https://www.bidease.com/privacy-policy
  • Manage ad preferences: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Opt out of personalized ads: https://optout.aboutads.info

4. Optional Local Notifications

Daily farm jokes are off by default. If you enable them, the app asks for Android notification permission and schedules up to one quiet message per day on your device. Notification content and schedules are not uploaded to NeonLabGames or Firebase. You can cancel all pending messages anytime from Farm Clicker Settings or Android notification settings.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Game progress and the random player ID are stored on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, game progress may also be backed up to your Google Play Games profile. Analytics and advertising records are processed by Google under its retention controls. Local progress, notification schedules, and the random player ID can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing app data. You may also contact us regarding an analytics data request.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data
  • Withdraw consent or choose "Do Not Sell or Share" where applicable from Settings → Ad privacy
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version.
